# Trip Expense App

- [ ] Foundation: Cloud, authentication, profiles, private schema and permissions.
- [ ] Trip creation, membership, secure invitations and join flow.
- [ ] Integer-money expense engine, atomic writes, receipts and financial tests.
- [ ] Ledger, member balances, partial settlements and suggestions.
- [ ] IndexedDB queue, idempotent synchronization and guarded offline PWA.
- [ ] Personal summaries, analytics, realtime and desktop/mobile verification.
- [ ] Email invitations: requires an owned, verified sender domain.
